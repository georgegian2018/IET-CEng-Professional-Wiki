# Start Here

Welcome to the **IET CEng Professional Wiki**.

This wiki is a structured, assessor-aligned knowledge base designed to support engineers preparing for **Chartered Engineer (CEng)** registration with the **Institution of Engineering and Technology (IET)** under the **UK-SPEC** framework.

---

## 🎯 Purpose of This Wiki

This resource helps you to:
- Understand what assessors expect
- Structure evidence clearly and professionally
- Write competence statements using STAR & SMART
- Prepare effectively for the Professional Review Interview (PRI)
- Maintain and present Continuing Professional Development (CPD)

---

## 👥 Who This Wiki Is For

- Engineers applying for **CEng**
- Engineers planning future professional registration
- IET mentors and reviewers
- Candidates preparing for PRI interviews

---

## ⚠️ Ethical Use

This wiki provides **guidance, structure, and examples**.

You must:
- Write your own evidence
- Base submissions on your own experience
- Avoid copying text verbatim

Assessors are trained to identify non-authentic submissions.
