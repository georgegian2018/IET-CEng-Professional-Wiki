# UK-SPEC Overview

This page provides a high-level overview of the **UK Standard for Professional Engineering Competence (UK-SPEC)** as applied to **Chartered Engineer (CEng)** registration with the Institution of Engineering and Technology (IET).

---

## What is UK-SPEC?

UK-SPEC defines the **competence and commitment requirements** for professional engineering registration in the UK.

For CEng, applicants must demonstrate:
- Advanced technical knowledge
- Application of engineering judgement
- Leadership and responsibility
- Professional and ethical commitment

---

## UK-SPEC Competence Structure

CEng competence is organised into **five areas**:

| Competence | Focus |
|-----------|------|
| **A** | Knowledge and understanding |
| **B** | Application of skills and problem solving |
| **C** | Leadership, management, and responsibility |
| **D** | Communication and interpersonal skills |
| **E** | Professional commitment |

Each competence is further divided into **sub-competences** (A1–E5).

---

## How Assessors Use UK-SPEC

Assessors:
- Look for **evidence**, not assertions
- Expect examples at **Chartered Engineer level**
- Evaluate **depth, responsibility, and judgement**
- Consider consistency across competences

One strong project may support multiple competences, but **each must be written separately**.

---

## Evidence Philosophy

Good UK-SPEC evidence:
- Is based on **real engineering work**
- Shows **decision-making and accountability**
- Demonstrates **impact and learning**
- Reflects **professional maturity**

Weak evidence typically:
- Describes team activity only
- Lists technologies without context
- Avoids responsibility
- Lacks outcomes
