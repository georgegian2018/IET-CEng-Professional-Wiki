# Competence C – Project Management and Leadership

## Purpose of Competence C

Competence C demonstrates your ability to **lead, manage, and deliver engineering work**.

This includes responsibility for people, resources, budgets, schedules, and risk.

---

## What Assessors Expect

You should demonstrate:
- Planning and control of work
- Leadership and coordination of others
- Risk, quality, and change management
- Accountability for outcomes

---

## Sub-Competences

- **C1** – Plan, budget, and organise tasks
- **C2** – Manage teams and resources
- **C3** – Manage quality and continuous improvement
- **C4** – Manage risk and uncertainty

---

## Writing Guidance

- Show leadership, not just participation
- Demonstrate decision-making authority
- Include outcomes and lessons learned
- Reflect on improvements made
