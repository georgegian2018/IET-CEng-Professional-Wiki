# 📄 Evidence Quality Checklist

---

Use this checklist **before submission** to assess the quality of each competence example.

---

## ✅ Structure & Clarity
- [ ] STAR structure is clear
- [ ] Your role is explicit
- [ ] Writing is concise and focused

## ✅ Technical Depth
- [ ] Engineering principles are evident
- [ ] Decisions are justified
- [ ] Constraints and trade-offs are discussed

## ✅ Professional Responsibility
- [ ] Ownership is demonstrated
- [ ] Risk, safety, or ethics are considered
- [ ] Standards or best practice are referenced

## ✅ Outcomes & Impact
- [ ] Results are stated
- [ ] Impact is measurable or evidenced
- [ ] Learning or improvement is shown

## ❌ Red Flags to Avoid
- Generic descriptions
- Management-only language without engineering
- Unverifiable claims
- Overly academic explanations

If you can tick **most boxes**, your evidence is likely assessor-ready.
