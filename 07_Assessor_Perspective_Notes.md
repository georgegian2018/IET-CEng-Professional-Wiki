# Assessor Perspective Notes

This page reflects how **IET assessors typically review competence evidence**.

---

## What Assessors Look For

- Clear ownership of engineering decisions
- Evidence of responsibility and accountability
- Depth appropriate to Chartered Engineer level
- Professional judgement, not task execution

---

## What Raises Concerns

- Overuse of “we”
- Vague leadership claims
- Missing outcomes
- Technical descriptions without decisions
- Copy-paste or templated language

---

## During the PRI Interview

Assessors may:
- Probe decision rationale
- Ask “what would you do differently?”
- Explore ethical or safety considerations
- Test ownership of claims

Prepare to **expand verbally** on written evidence.

---

## Key Insight

Assessors are not trying to fail candidates.  
They are verifying **professional readiness and integrity**.
