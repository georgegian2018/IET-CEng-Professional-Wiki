# Competence D – Communication and Interpersonal Skills

## Purpose of Competence D

Competence D demonstrates your ability to **communicate effectively and work with others** in an engineering context.

Communication must be **clear, appropriate, and influential**.

---

## What Assessors Expect

You should demonstrate:
- Effective written and verbal communication
- Engagement with technical and non-technical stakeholders
- Ability to influence, negotiate, and resolve issues
- Clear reporting and documentation

---

## Sub-Competences

- **D1** – Communicate effectively with others
- **D2** – Present and discuss proposals
- **D3** – Demonstrate personal and social skills

---

## Writing Guidance

- Identify the audience and purpose
- Highlight outcomes of communication
- Show adaptability of style and approach
- Avoid vague statements

---

## Next Steps
