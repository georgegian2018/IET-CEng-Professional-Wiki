# How to Use This Wiki

This wiki is designed to help engineers prepare **clear, structured, and assessor-aligned evidence** for IET Chartered Engineer (CEng) registration under the UK-SPEC framework.

It should be used **systematically**, not read end-to-end like a book.

---

## 🔁 Recommended Workflow

Follow this sequence for best results:

1. Read **Start Here** to understand intent, ethics, and expectations  
2. Review the **UK-SPEC Overview** to understand the competence framework  
3. Study **How IET Competences Are Written (The Framework)**  
4. Complete the **Competence Evidence Workbook (CEng i4)**  
5. Use the **STAR & SMART Templates** to draft evidence  
6. Navigate to each **Competence Overview (A–E)**  
7. Write and refine **A1–E5** evidence  
8. Validate using the **Evidence Quality Checklist**  
9. Prepare for the interview using the **PRI Preparation Guide**

---

## 🧭 Navigating the Wiki

- Use the **Sidebar** for structured navigation  
- Each competence area (A–E) contains:
  - An **Overview (_Index.md)** explaining assessor expectations
  - Individual sub-competence pages (A1–E5)
- Core guidance pages apply **across all competences**

---

## ✍ Writing Good Evidence

When drafting competence statements:

- Focus on **your personal role and responsibility**
- Explain **decisions**, not just tasks
- Reference standards, methods, or frameworks where relevant
- Quantify outcomes where possible
- Reflect on learning and improvement

Avoid generic descriptions or purely managerial language.

---

## 🎤 Using This Wiki for PRI Preparation

Your written evidence should act as:
- A **prompt** for interview answers
- A **memory aid** for projects and decisions
- A **structure** for explaining your role clearly

Be prepared to expand verbally on:
- Why decisions were made
- Alternatives considered
- Risks, ethics, and lessons learned

---

## 🔎 Final Advice

Quality matters more than volume.  
Clarity matters more than complexity.

Use this wiki as a **professional tool**, not a shortcut.
