## UK-SPEC Statement
Apply appropriate theoretical and practical methods to the analysis and solution
of engineering problems.

## Action

- Identification of requirements and constraints
- Selection of methods, tools, and techniques
- Evaluation of options and trade-offs
- Implementation of the chosen solution
- Testing, validation, or commissioning activities

## Result

- Performance, reliability, or efficiency improvements
- Risk reduction or cost savings
- Compliance with requirements or standards
