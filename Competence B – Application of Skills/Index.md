# Competence B – Application of Skills

## Purpose of Competence B

Competence B demonstrates your ability to **apply engineering knowledge and skills** to deliver effective solutions.

Assessors want to see **practical engineering competence**, not theoretical discussion.

---

## What Assessors Expect

You should demonstrate:
- Problem identification and analysis
- Development and implementation of solutions
- Technical judgement and decision-making
- Consideration of safety, quality, cost, and risk

---

## Sub-Competences

- **B1** – Identify, review, and select techniques and procedures
- **B2** – Contribute to the design, development, and implementation
- **B3** – Implement design solutions and evaluate effectiveness

---

## Writing Guidance

- Focus on **your personal contribution**
- Explain trade-offs and constraints
- Quantify results where possible
- Show ownership of technical decisions

---

## Next Steps

➡ Proceed to **B1–B3**, using real projects as primary evidence.
