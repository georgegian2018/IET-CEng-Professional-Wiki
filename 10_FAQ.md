# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How many examples per competence?
Quality matters more than quantity. Strong examples can cover multiple sub-competences.

---

### Can one project cover multiple competences?
Yes, if clearly mapped and written separately.

---

### Should I reference standards?
Yes, where relevant, but focus on application, not citation.

---

### What level of detail is expected?
Sufficient to demonstrate judgement, responsibility, and impact.

---

### Can I reuse wording?
Avoid reuse. Each competence should be written independently.
