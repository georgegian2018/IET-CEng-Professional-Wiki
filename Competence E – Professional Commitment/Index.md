# Competence E – Professional Commitment

## Purpose of Competence E

Competence E demonstrates your **commitment to professional standards, ethics, and development**.

This competence often differentiates strong applications.

---

## What Assessors Expect

You should demonstrate:
- Ethical behaviour and professional integrity
- Commitment to safety, sustainability, and public interest
- Engagement with CPD
- Professional involvement and responsibility

---

## Sub-Competences

- **E1** – Comply with codes of conduct and ethics
- **E2** – Manage and apply CPD
- **E3** – Promote safe systems of work
- **E4** – Promote equality, diversity, and inclusion
- **E5** – Understand and apply sustainability principles

---

## Writing Guidance

- Reference professional standards explicitly
- Show reflection and learning
- Avoid generic statements
- Demonstrate proactive commitment

---

## Next Steps
