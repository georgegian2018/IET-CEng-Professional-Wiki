# STAR & SMART Templates for Competence Writing

This page provides structured templates for writing **high-quality, assessor-aligned competence evidence**.

All competence statements should follow **STAR** and satisfy **SMART**.

---

## ⭐ STAR Framework

### S — Situation
- Context of the work
- Engineering environment
- Constraints and drivers

### T — Task
- Your responsibility
- Objectives you were accountable for
- What success looked like

### A — Action
- What YOU did
- Decisions you made
- Methods, standards, tools used
- Alternatives considered

### R — Result
- Outcomes achieved
- Measurable impact
- Lessons learned
- Improvements made

---

## 🎯 SMART Criteria

Each example should be:

- **Specific** – Clear scope and role
- **Measurable** – Quantified where possible
- **Achievable** – Realistic and credible
- **Relevant** – Directly mapped to the competence
- **Time-bound** – Linked to a period or project phase

---

## ✍ Generic STAR Template

```text
Situation:
Brief description of the engineering context and challenge.

Task:
Your role, responsibility, and objectives.

Action:
Detailed explanation of decisions, methods, standards, and leadership.

Result:
Outcomes, metrics, impact, and learning.
