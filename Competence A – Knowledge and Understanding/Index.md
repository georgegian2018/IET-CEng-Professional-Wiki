# Competence A – Knowledge and Understanding

## Purpose of Competence A

Competence A demonstrates that you **maintain and extend a sound theoretical and technical understanding** relevant to your engineering role.

Assessors are looking for:
- Depth of knowledge (not just awareness)
- Application of theory to real engineering problems
- Evidence of learning, development, and currency

---

## What Assessors Expect

You should demonstrate:
- Use of engineering principles, standards, and methodologies
- Understanding of limitations, assumptions, and risks
- Continuous professional learning (formal and informal)

Evidence should show **how knowledge informed decisions**, not just that you possess knowledge.

---

## Sub-Competences

- **A1** – Maintain and extend a sound theoretical approach  
- **A2** – Use a combination of general and specialist engineering knowledge

Each sub-competence should be supported by **clear examples** where theory influenced outcomes.

---

## Writing Guidance

- Reference recognised standards, methods, or frameworks
- Explain *why* a technical approach was selected
- Avoid textbook explanations unless directly applied
- Show progression over time
